## 1. Right to Data Erasure & Account Deletion

At **MAD Entertainment LLC** ("MAD Entertainment," "we," "our"), we respect your autonomy over your personal data. In accordance with United States federal standards and applicable state privacy frameworks (including CCPA/CPRA, VCDPA, CPA, and others), you have the right to request the deletion of your personal information and the permanent closure of your user account.

---

## 2. What Data Is Deleted Upon Request

When your account deletion request is verified and executed, we permanently remove or cryptographically anonymize:
* **Account Credentials**: Name, email address, password hashes, mobile phone number, and Google OAuth associations.
* **Profile Metadata**: Saved preferences, communication subscription records, and search histories.
* **Support Records**: Non-essential customer service tickets, feedback submissions, and uploaded attachments.

---

## 3. Statutory Exceptions & Retained Records

In compliance with United States legal, financial, and regulatory obligations, certain limited data must be retained even after an account deletion request is processed:
* **Financial & Tax Records**: Transaction receipts, sales tax calculations, and Stripe payment reference IDs are retained for **seven (7) years** to satisfy IRS and state department of revenue audit requirements.
* **Active Event Admission Passes**: If you hold active tickets for an upcoming event, your ticket data cannot be deleted until the event has concluded or the tickets have been refunded.
* **Fraud Prevention & Security Logs**: Anonymized IP connection logs and chargeback history retained strictly to prevent fraud and protect platform integrity.

---

## 4. Step-by-Step Deletion Request Procedure

You can submit an account or data deletion request through either of the following channels:

### Option A: In-App Self-Service
1. Log into your account at [https://www.madentertainments.net/dashboard](https://www.madentertainments.net/dashboard).
2. Navigate to **Account Settings** $\longrightarrow$ **Privacy & Security**.
3. Click **"Request Account Deletion"** and confirm via the OTP verification sent to your registered email or phone.

### Option B: Direct Privacy Team Request
Send an email to [privacy@madentertainments.net](mailto:privacy@madentertainments.net) with the subject line: **"Data Deletion Request"**. Please include:
* Your full name and registered account email address.
* Confirmation of whether you wish to delete specific records or delete your entire account.

---

## 5. Verification & Processing Timelines

* **Identity Verification**: To protect your account against malicious or unauthorized erasure, we will verify your identity via email OTP or security confirmation before executing the deletion.
* **Processing Period**: Deletion requests are processed within **thirty (30) calendar days** of identity verification. You will receive a final confirmation email once all eligible data has been permanently removed from our primary production databases.
