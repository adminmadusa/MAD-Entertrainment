## 1. Managing Your Communication Channels

At **MAD Entertainment LLC** ("MAD Entertainment," "we," "our"), we provide transparent, granular control over how and when we communicate with you across email, SMS text messaging, and web push notifications in the **United States**.

---

## 2. Categories of Communications

We send two distinct categories of electronic communications:

### A. Essential Transactional & Security Notices (Mandatory)
These communications are required to administer your account and fulfill ticket purchases:
* Digital ticket receipts, payment confirmation invoices, and QR code access passes.
* Security One-Time Passwords (OTPs) and account password resets.
* Critical operational alerts regarding event cancellations, venue relocations, or emergency postponements.
* *Note: Because these messages are essential to fulfilling your paid orders, you cannot opt out of transactional notices while holding active tickets or an open account.*

### B. Promotional & Marketing Updates (Optional / Opt-In)
* Announcements for upcoming concert tours, featured DJ nights, and exclusive early-bird presale codes.
* Curated entertainment digests and city-specific event recommendations.
* Special seasonal promotional discounts and VIP ticket upgrades.

---

## 3. How to Update Your Preferences

You can customize your communication preferences at any time through the following channels:

### 1. In-App Preference Center
* Log into your account at [https://www.madentertainments.net/dashboard](https://www.madentertainments.net/dashboard).
* Go to **Account Settings** $\longrightarrow$ **Notifications & Communication Preferences**.
* Toggle your preferred email and SMS subscription channels on or off.

### 2. Email Unsubscribe Links
* Every promotional or newsletter email sent by MAD Entertainment includes a single-click **"Unsubscribe"** link in the footer. Clicking this link immediately removes your email address from that marketing list.

### 3. SMS Opt-Out
* Reply **"STOP"** to any marketing or promotional text message to instantly terminate SMS notifications.

---

## 4. Processing Time & Support Assistance

* Email and SMS opt-out requests are processed automatically and take effect within twenty-four (24) hours.
* If you continue to receive unwanted communications after updating your preferences, please contact our support team at [privacy@madentertainments.net](mailto:privacy@madentertainments.net) and we will manually review your account.
