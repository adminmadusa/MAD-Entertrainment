## 1. Introduction & Operating Territory

Welcome to **MAD Entertainment** ("MAD," "we," "our," or "us"). We operate a digital ticketing and live event management platform located at [https://www.madentertainments.net](https://www.madentertainments.net). 

This Privacy Policy applies exclusively to individuals residing in the **United States** who access our websites, mobile applications, services, and event booking interfaces (collectively, the "Platform"). **MAD Entertainment operates and offers ticketing services solely within the United States.**

We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting your personal information through transparent data practices. This policy details how we collect, store, share, and protect your information, as well as your rights under applicable US federal and state laws.

---

## 2. Information We Collect

We collect information directly from you when you interact with our Platform, automatically as you navigate our pages, and from trusted third-party integrations:

### A. Information You Provide Directly
* **Account Registration & Profiles**: Name, email address, mobile phone number, postal address, and profile preferences.
* **Authentication Credentials**: Password hashes or single sign-on (SSO) identifiers when logging in via Google Authentication.
* **Event Bookings & Orders**: Attendee names, ticket tier selections, transaction amounts, and coupon redemption codes.
* **Customer Support Inquiries**: Details provided when submitting support requests, identity verification documents, or feedback.

### B. Payment & Financial Data
When purchasing tickets, your payment card details (card number, expiration date, CVV, billing zip code) are processed directly by our PCI-DSS Level 1 certified payment processor, **Stripe**. **MAD Entertainment never stores, views, or retains your raw credit card numbers or security codes.** We retain only non-sensitive transaction metadata, such as transaction reference IDs, the last four digits of your card, and payment status records.

### C. Automatically Collected Technical Data
* **Device & Connection Data**: IP address, browser type and version, operating system, device identifiers, and network connection details.
* **Usage & Log Data**: Pages viewed, access timestamps, referral URLs, checkout progression events, and crash diagnostics collected via telemetry tools.

---

## 3. How We Use Your Information

We process personal information strictly for legitimate commercial and operational purposes:
1. **Fulfilling Orders & Issuing Tickets**: Generating unique QR code passes, sending digital confirmation receipts, and managing event admission.
2. **Account Administration & Security**: Verifying user identities via One-Time Passwords (OTP), authenticating sessions, and safeguarding against fraudulent bookings.
3. **Event Updates & Critical Notifications**: Delivering timely event alerts, venue changes, gate opening times, or postponement notices via email and SMS.
4. **Platform Optimization**: Analyzing user experience bottlenecks, improving interface performance, and resolving software defects.
5. **Legal & Financial Compliance**: Complying with applicable US state tax laws, record-keeping mandates, and enforcing our Terms of Service.

We **do not sell your personal information** to third-party data brokers, nor do we share your personal data for cross-context behavioral advertising.

## 5. Data Retention & Storage Safeguards

We implement administrative, technical, and physical safeguards designed to protect personal information against unauthorized access, loss, alteration, or disclosure:
* **Transport Encryption**: All web and API traffic is encrypted in transit using Transport Layer Security (TLS 1.3).
* **Storage Protection**: User databases are hosted in encrypted-at-rest environments with strict role-based access control (RBAC).
* **Retention Schedule**: Personal data linked to accounts is retained as long as your account remains active. Transaction and ticketing records are retained for seven (7) years to satisfy US federal and state tax, accounting, and anti-fraud statutory requirements.

## 6. Your Privacy Rights & Choices

Depending on your US state of residence (including California, Virginia, Colorado, Connecticut, Texas, and Utah), you possess specific privacy rights:
* **Right to Access**: You may request details on the categories and specific pieces of personal information we maintain about you.
* **Right to Correction**: You can update or correct inaccuracies in your profile directly in your account dashboard.
* **Right to Deletion**: You may request the deletion of your personal data, subject to legal and financial retention obligations.
* **Right to Opt-Out**: You can opt out of promotional emails by clicking "Unsubscribe" or manage text notifications by replying "STOP" to any SMS.

To exercise any of these rights, please review our [Data Deletion Policy](/legal/data-deletion) or contact our privacy team at [privacy@madentertainments.net](mailto:privacy@madentertainments.net).
