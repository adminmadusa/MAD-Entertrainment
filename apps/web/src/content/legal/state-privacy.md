## 1. Multi-State Privacy Notice

This United States State Privacy Rights Notice supplements the main [Privacy Policy](/legal/privacy) of **MAD Entertainment LLC** ("MAD Entertainment," "we," "our") and provides additional disclosures for residents of states with comprehensive consumer privacy statutes, including:

* **Virginia** (Virginia Consumer Data Protection Act - VCDPA)
* **Colorado** (Colorado Privacy Act - CPA)
* **Connecticut** (Connecticut Data Privacy Act - CTDPA)
* **Texas** (Texas Data Privacy and Security Act - TDPSA)
* **Oregon** (Oregon Consumer Privacy Act - OCPA)
* **Utah** (Utah Consumer Privacy Act - UCPA)
* And other US states enacting comprehensive consumer data privacy legislation.

---

## 2. Summary of Consumer Privacy Rights

Residents of eligible US states possess statutory rights regarding the personal information collected by MAD Entertainment:

1. **Right to Confirm & Access**: You have the right to confirm whether we process your personal data and to obtain a copy of that data in a portable, readily usable format.
2. **Right to Correct**: You have the right to correct inaccuracies in your personal data, taking into account the nature and purpose of the processing.
3. **Right to Delete**: You have the right to request deletion of personal data provided by or obtained about you, subject to legal and financial retention requirements.
4. **Right to Opt-Out of Targeted Advertising & Profiling**: We do not sell personal data, nor do we engage in targeted cross-site advertising or automated profiling with legal effects.
5. **Right to Non-Discrimination**: We will not discriminate against any consumer for exercising their statutory privacy rights.

## 3. Submitting a State Privacy Request & Appeals Process

### A. Submitting a Request
To submit a verified consumer request:
* Send an email to [privacy@madentertainments.net](mailto:privacy@madentertainments.net) with the subject line **"US State Privacy Request"**.
* State your state of residence, full name, and registered account email.

### B. Appeals Process
If we decline to take action on your request, we will notify you within forty-five (45) days with our written explanation. Residents of Virginia, Colorado, Connecticut, Texas, and Oregon have the right to appeal our decision within thirty (30) days by emailing [privacy@madentertainments.net](mailto:privacy@madentertainments.net) with the subject **"Privacy Decision Appeal"**. 

If your appeal is denied, you may contact your state Attorney General's Office of Consumer Protection.
