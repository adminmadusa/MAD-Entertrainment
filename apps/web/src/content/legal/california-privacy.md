## 4. California Consumer Rights

Under the CCPA/CPRA, California residents have the following enforceable rights:
1. **Right to Know / Access**: The right to request disclosure of the categories and specific pieces of personal data collected about you, the categories of sources, and the business purposes for collecting it.
2. **Right to Delete**: The right to request the deletion of your personal information, subject to statutory exceptions (such as completed financial records and tax compliance).
3. **Right to Correct**: The right to request the correction of inaccurate personal information maintained in your profile.
4. **Right to Limit Sensitive Personal Information**: We only use sensitive personal information (such as login credentials) for strictly necessary operational and security functions; therefore, opt-out limitations are not applicable.
5. **Right to Non-Discrimination**: We will never deny services, charge different ticket prices, or provide a different quality of service because you exercised your California privacy rights.
